## Real flight

`artifacts/trials/20260923-122157-reanchor`, flown from this commit:

    uv run python scripts/cube_trial.py --real --confirm --pose reanchor \
        --site docs/wp5-flight3-site.json --tag-map docs/wp5-flight3-map.json \
        --mission docs/wp5-flight3-demo.json --min-battery 20 \
        --detect gemini-openrouter --detect-tiles 2x2 \
        --consent-db artifacts/consent.sqlite3 --location home-office --date 2026-09-22 \
        --i-authorise-paid-calls

Took off at 51 % battery and 76-77 C (42 % right after takeoff).

|---|---|---|

- **Ended early.** At 10 % battery the Tello answered `error Auto land` to the RETURN correction and
  landed itself. The mission's own status is a failure; every stop before it was reached and
  localised. The operator judged the gate passed on 2026-09-23.
- **Cause: too little battery for the mission**, not the code. 51 % at takeoff, 32 points consumed;
  the same mission took 24 points the day before. Fly this mission from 80 % or more.
- **Live detection with 2 x 2 crops** ran on all 5 captured frames, no `PROVIDER_ERROR`. Sentinel saw
  one person (high) at FAR_L_HI, NEAR_L_LO and FAR_MID_HI, nothing elsewhere. No crop-only find on
  these frames, so tiling neither added nor cost anything here. Checked independently against the
  saved frames: every box re-derived from the raw `box_2d` matches the stored pixel box to within
  1 px, none degenerate or outside the frame, and the two frames with no person truly have none.
- **No `Not joystick` refusal** at 50 cm/s: the settle pause holds.
- **The vertical check alerted at every stop**, and `height?` is the reading that is wrong. The
  mismatches were FAR_L_HI +28.8, NEAR_R_LO +27.9, FAR_R_HI +41.2, NEAR_L_LO +32.0,
  FAR_MID_HI +33.7, RETURN -44.2 cm. The five positive ones are tightly grouped; RETURN's burst was
  taken while the drone was already auto-landing (implied-height spread 83 cm within the burst,
  against 3-8 cm at every other stop), so it measured the descent.

  Three corrections to what this document first claimed. It fired six times, not three. The signs
  are not inconsistent, once the auto-landing burst is set aside. And the method is not a noisy
  triangulation: `_solve_joint` derives each tag's distance from the same PnP solve that produces
  the fix, so `tag_implied_height_cm` algebraically recovers that solve's own camera height -- the
  two tags of one report agree to a median of 0.007 cm (60 reports), and repeatability within a
  burst is 3-8 cm, far better than the +-10 cm the helper's docstring claims.

  A third method, independent of tag range and apparent size (the tags' pixel elevation below the
  optical axis, the measured 8.6 deg pitch, and the horizontal distance the flight already trusts),
  agrees with the tags to within 2-3 cm at two stops and never with the barometer. No single tag
  height and no single pitch angle reconcile the numbers.

  The residual is not constant: about 33-37 cm at the 130 cm stops and 48-51 cm at the 70/80 cm
  ones, so a single `altitude_bias_cm` cannot model it (and `height?` is quantised to 10 cm before
  the offset is added). Consequences: the cube's z bounds are enforced against `height?` plus the
  offset, so the real ceiling margin was 35-50 cm smaller than the site file implies -- no breach
  this flight, the tags stayed visible -- and `VERTICAL_MISMATCH_CM = 25` was chosen to clear a
  noise band that does not exist. Next step: hover at a commanded height with the tags in view and
  tape-measure the camera (`docs/wp5-altitude-tape-test.json`).
- **No landing temperature:** the drone stopped answering after it landed itself. There is no
  temperature guard anywhere in the cube code; the Tello's own firmware is the only protection.

## What this gate does not cover

- **Objects.** The counting chain is proved on rendered scenes and on a phone and a person in the
  room, never on the laptops the demo is about.
- **The live judge and the live agent.** Both were exercised earlier with paid calls; this gate ran
  the offline stand-ins only.
- **The survey flying itself.** `CubeFlight` is dry-run here. The survey has never flown its own
  sweep with a live detector.
- **The altitude bias.** `altitude_bias_cm: 18` is too small and the wrong shape: this flight's own
  check says the drone was 28-41 cm above `height?` + 18, by two optically independent methods, with
  a residual that grows as the commanded height falls. Nothing above is invalidated -- the tags
  stayed visible and no bound was breached -- but the site's vertical model is not yet right, and
  `docs/wp5-altitude-tape-test.json` is the measurement that settles it.
- **Tag coverage above 140 cm.** With the tags at 53 cm, every cell of the flight3 cube is blind
  above roughly that height. Flying higher needs tags mounted higher, or a second row.
- **Objects on the floor near the tag wall.** At a low near stop the frame's bottom edge cuts across
  the foot of the doors, so an object standing there is half out of shot (09-22 NEAR_R_LO). No
  detector setting recovers that; the stop has to be higher or further back.
