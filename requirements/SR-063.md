Amended at consent (2026-09-21, Colin Aubé, coherence-alignment session: "Yes I consent
to C1, I consent for reanchor mode") with the replacement statement proposed in the WP5
cube design section 5 C1. Reason: without it the runner navigates on a dead-reckoned
position that is never refreshed, so error accumulates leg after leg. Targets still come
only from the approved mission. The blast radius of a detector fault is now at worst a
misplaced start of one leg, and only after the quality gate, the plausibility gate, the
envelope and containment have all passed. AC-1 still holds literally: runner.py consumes
no pose data; re-anchoring lives in sentinel.cube.mission.
